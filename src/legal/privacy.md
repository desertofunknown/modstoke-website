# Privacy Policy

What Modstoke collects, why, how long we keep it, and what you can ask us to do with it.

Effective 12 November 2026

## Who we are and what this covers {#scope}

Modstoke is run by OpenACAI Inc ("OpenACAI", "we", "us"), a 501(c)(3) nonprofit based in Farmington, Missouri. This policy covers modstoke.com, your Modstoke account and the hosting panel at panel.modstoke.com, the game servers we host, our mods and tools, and donations made through the site.

Our Discord apps, Sentinel and Radio, are covered by the [Discord app privacy page](/apps/privacy/). OpenACAI's other services have [their own policy](https://openacai.com/privacy-policy/).

The short version:

- We collect what we need to run accounts and servers and to keep them safe.
- We don't sell personal information, show ads, or use your data to train AI models.
- You can ask us for a copy of your information, or to correct or delete it.

## Visiting the site {#site}

You can read modstoke.com and download our mods and tools without an account. The site sets no advertising or tracking cookies.

Like any website, ours receives your IP address, browser details and the pages you ask for. Cloudflare sits in front of our sites to deliver them and block attacks, so it sees that traffic too, and it gives us visit counts in total that don't identify you. We keep server logs to run and secure the service.

## Your account {#account}

When you make an account or sign in, we keep:

- your username and email address, and a hash of your password if you set one;
- **Discord**, if you sign in with it: your Discord ID and the email on your Discord account;
- **Steam**, if you link it: your SteamID, and whether that account owns Sons of the Forest, which we check with Steam when you link and from time to time afterwards. Owner and staff rights on a server go by SteamID;
- **Nexus Mods**, if you link it: your Nexus name, avatar and whether you have Premium, plus the access tokens Nexus gives us, which we store encrypted;
- what you tell us when you apply for a server or ask for help;
- your settings for the desktop, such as theme and layout, so they follow you between devices.

### Sign-in records {#records}

For security, and so we can answer a lawful request, we record each sign-up, sign-in, failed sign-in and sign-out on the panel and over SFTP, with:

- the time and the method used;
- your IP address, the network name it resolves to, and the country Cloudflare reports for it;
- your browser, operating system and a random ID we keep in a cookie to tell one browser from another.

We keep these records for 365 days. Only our senior administrators can open them, and each time one does, that is logged too. If the law requires us to preserve an account's records, we keep them until that ends.

## Hosted servers {#servers}

For a server you rent or run on a trial, we keep:

- everything on the server: the game's files, your world, mods, settings and whatever you upload;
- backups, for as long as your plan says, and files you delete, which stay in the Trash for 7 days;
- how the server is doing: CPU, memory, disk and network use, player counts and installed mods, sampled every 10 minutes and kept for 30 days;
- the console log, events and crash reports, so that you and we can see what went wrong;
- virus scan results for uploads and mod folders: the file's path, size, fingerprint and verdict;
- who on your staff did what, in the server's activity log, kept for 365 days;
- who has the server's desktop open right now, shown to the server's other staff.

Our staff can open a server's files, console and logs to run the service, to help when you ask, to look into abuse or a security problem, and when the law requires it.

### People who play on a hosted server {#players}

A server we host records the people who join it. The server's owner decides who plays there and who their staff are, and we keep this for them and to keep the service safe:

- Steam name and SteamID, and when each player joined and left;
- chat, kept for 30 days;
- where players are in the world while they play. Positions are live only and are gone within a few minutes;
- moderation: mutes, jails, kicks and bans, the whitelist, and notes staff write about a player;
- if a player uses the Modstoke Client mod, the health and inventory their game reports.

Only that server's owner and staff, and our own staff, can see this. A public server listing shows the server's name and how many are playing, never who.

The record of a visit is kept for a year after it ended. When a server is deleted, everything it held about its players is deleted with it.

If you play on a server and want to know what it holds about you, or want it removed sooner, ask the server's owner, or ask us.

### Ember {#ember}

Ember, the helper in the desktop, answers using a model that runs on our own machine. Your questions and the facts about your server that go with them aren't sent to an outside AI company and aren't used to train models.

## Mods and tools {#tools}

The Modstoke Manager and Loader fetch release information, catalogs and downloads from modstoke.com, and from Nexus Mods or sotf-mods.com when you use those stores. Those requests carry your IP address, as any download does. If you sign in to Nexus Mods in the Manager, that is between you and Nexus.

## Donations and payments {#payments}

Payments go through PayPal. We never see or store your card or bank details. For a donation we keep PayPal's order or subscription ID, the amount, its status and PayPal's ID for the payer. When paid hosting opens, we will keep the same for a plan, along with which plan and server it pays for.

## Cookies and what's kept in your browser {#cookies}

The panel uses only what it needs to work:

- a session cookie that keeps you signed in, and a token that protects forms;
- `modstoke_device`, a random ID kept for two years, used only in the sign-in records above;
- your desktop's settings and window layout, kept in your browser's storage;
- an offline copy of a server's desktop, if you install it as an app. Signing out clears it;
- a notification subscription, if you turn alerts on. Alerts are delivered by your browser's own push service, run by Google, Mozilla, Apple or Microsoft, and they arrive encrypted.

There are no advertising cookies.

## How we use information {#use}

We use what we collect to:

- provide accounts, servers and downloads, and fix them when they break;
- keep people and servers safe: stop abuse, cheating and break-ins, and enforce our [terms](/terms/);
- tell you about your server, such as a trial ending or a problem with it;
- take payments and donations, and keep the records the law asks a nonprofit to keep;
- understand, in totals, how the service is used so we can improve it.

Where the law asks for a legal basis, ours are: carrying out our agreement with you, our legitimate interest in running a safe service, your consent where we ask for it, and our legal duties.

## Who we share it with {#sharing}

We don't sell personal information. We share it only:

- with the services that help us run Modstoke, for that purpose: Cloudflare (delivery, protection and visit counts), InterServer (the datacentre in New Jersey where our machine lives), PayPal (payments), Discord and Steam (sign-in and linking), and Nexus Mods and sotf-mods.com (when you use their stores);
- with a server's owner and staff, for the players and activity on that server;
- when the law requires it, or when it's needed to protect someone's safety or our rights;
- with an organisation that takes over Modstoke, under this same policy.

## How long we keep it {#retention}

| What | How long |
|---|---|
| Account details | Until you ask us to delete the account |
| Sign-in records | 365 days |
| Server usage samples | 30 days |
| Chat on a hosted server | 30 days |
| Player positions | Minutes |
| Player sessions on a hosted server | A year after the session ended |
| Moderation records, the whitelist and staff notes | Until the server's staff remove them |
| Everything a hosted server holds about its players | Deleted when the server is deleted |
| A server's activity log | 365 days |
| Backups | 3, 7 or 30 days, by plan |
| Trash on a server | 7 days |
| A server's files after it ends | 14 days, then deleted |
| A trial's world after the server is deleted | 30 days |
| Donation and payment records | As long as tax and accounting rules require |

Anything under a legal hold is kept until the hold ends.

## Keeping it safe {#security}

Everything travels over HTTPS. Each customer's server runs in its own space, apart from the others and from the panel. Passwords are stored hashed and linked-account tokens encrypted. Access to sign-in records is limited and logged. No system is perfectly secure, and if a breach affects your information we will tell you.

## Your choices and rights {#rights}

You can do these yourself where the panel lets you, or ask us to:

- get a copy of the information we hold about you;
- correct it, or delete your account and what goes with it;
- unlink Discord, Steam or Nexus Mods;
- download your world from the panel at any time;
- turn notifications off, or clear what's stored in your browser.

Email legal@openacai.com or ask in [our Discord](https://discord.gg/T2Tjh5ECS). We may need to confirm it's you, and we may keep what the law requires or what we need to deal with abuse. Depending on where you live, the law may give you further rights, including the right to complain to your data protection authority. We'll honour those.

## Where it's kept {#where}

Our machine is in New Jersey, in the United States, and your information is processed there. If you use Modstoke from another country, your information comes to the United States.

## Children {#children}

Modstoke isn't for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, tell us and we'll remove it. People aged 13 to 17 need a parent or guardian's permission.

## Things we're still building {#planned}

Some things we've announced aren't live yet. When they arrive, this is how they'll work, and we'll update this page before each one launches:

- **Paid plans.** PayPal will take the payment. We'll keep the records described under Donations and payments.
- **Email.** We don't send email today. When we start, it will be for things about your account and server, not marketing.
- **Backups to your own cloud storage.** If you connect Google Drive or OneDrive, we'll store the access you grant and send your backups there, and nothing else.
- **Discord channels for a server.** If you turn them on, your server's announcements, and chat if you bridge it, will be posted to Discord.
- **A store for mods, themes and apps.** Installing something will tell its source that a download happened, as any download does.
- **Voice and radio on larger plans.** We'll say exactly what is and isn't recorded before they open.

## Changes {#changes}

When we change this policy we post the new version here with its date. If a change matters, we announce it in our Discord or on the panel before it takes effect.
